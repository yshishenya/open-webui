# PAYG and feedback email behavior

The existing durable queue reconstructs `topup_credited` only from succeeded YooKassa topups with a matching applied ledger entry. Credit matching includes owner, wallet, reference, positive amount and currency. It is independent of optional product consent, but requires an active account and suitable verified address without bounce/complaint suppression. An unavailable address does not remove the account's history or change money.

`credited_v1/topup_credited` states the exact amount in minor units and links to the authenticated topup history. It is a service notice, not a subscription confirmation, marketing message or fiscal receipt. The old subscription-only payment_confirmation template is not used for PAYG. Reconciliation restores a missed queue insert after billing commits; the unique payment scenario key prevents a repeated callback from creating another notice. Delivery failure does not roll back credit.

`payment_help_72h` selects the latest provider-confirmed canceled attempt in its existing three-to-seven-day window. A provider ID and recorded final cancellation are required. Local failed/create_failed, missing provider state, pending/creating/waiting_for_capture or success without a matching credit never establish a rejected payment. Such unresolved recent attempts block the general paid invitation; they follow normal billing reconciliation, without email code inventing a final status or initiating payment. A matching credit applied at or after the failed attempt cancels help even when that successful Payment was created earlier.

`paid_value_72h` requires a server success and no confirmed credit; a suitable help attempt or unresolved recent attempt takes priority. This template links to the current guide's cost explanation and public calculation; it makes no numerical price or model superiority claim. The separately checked paid example and guide enhancement remain rollout gates before a broad B pilot. Images are excluded until model availability, route and rates are separately verified.

`feedback_14d` asks one main question depending on current foreground success: what worked/what is missing, or what prevented the first task. Its second brief question distinguishes credited from free usage. Reply-To uses configured support; no separate survey or registration is required.

Before external DATA, the queue rechecks account, address, consent, scenario and frequency, then reconstructs content from current server facts. If it differs from the prepared content (including recipient name, exact credit or feedback variant), the job returns to pending with reason `content_changed`, without submitting DATA. The next claim renders current content. Content is held only in memory; queue rows and administrative exports contain no email bodies. This final check describes the state at that gate, not an impossible guarantee about facts changing after the transaction ends.

Existing rolling product frequency, help weekly frequency, leases, shared SMTP reserve and unknown-submission rules still apply. No blind resend for unknown, no reuse of checkout links, no automatic payment, no payment-state mutation from email decisions. Real 24-hour/72-hour/fourteen-day calendar acceptance and human feedback require separate pilot evidence.
